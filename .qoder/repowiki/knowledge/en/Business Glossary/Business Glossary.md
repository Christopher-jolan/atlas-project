### Call Analysis
- Definition：The per-call AI-generated record stored in the `call_analyses` table, containing fields such as department, agent info, customer phone/name, call direction/duration, transcript, JSONB analysis payload, purchase intent score, satisfaction score, agent quality score, ticket priority flag, and whether human review is needed.
- Aliases：call_analysis、call_analyses

### Monthly Report
- Definition：Aggregated summary of call analyses for a given month and department, persisted in the `monthly_reports` table as a JSONB payload along with total call count. Generated by the `atlas-call-intelligence-monthly-report.json` n8n workflow and browsable via the panel's `/monthly-reports` route.
- Aliases：monthly_reports

### Atlas Manager Panel
- Definition：The FastAPI-based admin dashboard (port 8080) that renders Jinja2 HTML templates to visualize call analyses and monthly reports. Pages include dashboard, top performers, ready-to-buy leads, unhappy customers, staff performance, call duration, satisfaction, successful sales, monthly reports detail, and AI insights. Access is protected by a simple password cookie when `PANEL_PASSWORD` is set.
- Aliases：panel、admin panel

### Atlas Call Intelligence
- Definition：The product name for the voice-call analysis pipeline: ingest audio or transcript via n8n webhook, transcribe if needed, run LLM-based sales analysis, persist results to PostgreSQL, and surface them through the Manager Panel and email notifications.
- Aliases：Call Intelligence、Atlas Voice Analysis
