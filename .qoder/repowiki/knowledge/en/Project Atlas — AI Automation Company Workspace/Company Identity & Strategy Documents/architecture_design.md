Two top-level document folders organize strategic content by scope: `00_Company/` holds foundational identity artifacts (Mission, Vision, Values, Goals, Brand, Services), while `01_Strategy/` captures go-to-market and operational strategy (BusinessModel, Competitors, ICP, KPI, PainPoints, Pricing, Roadmap). Each file is a standalone markdown document with no cross-file imports or code dependencies; structure is purely filesystem-based and ordered numerically to reflect hierarchy from identity outward to execution.
