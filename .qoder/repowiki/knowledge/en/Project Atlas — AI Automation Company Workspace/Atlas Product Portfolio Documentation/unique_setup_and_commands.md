Call Intelligence v1 is deployed by running `cd docker && docker compose up -d`, then importing two n8n workflows (`atlas-call-intelligence-v1.json`, `atlas-call-intelligence-monthly-report.json`) into the n8n panel at `http://localhost:5678`, creating an Atlas PostgreSQL credential, and optionally configuring SMTP. A monthly report cron runs on the first day of each month at 8 AM Asia/Tehran.
